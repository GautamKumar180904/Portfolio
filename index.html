I'm Gautam Kumar, a video editor, graphic designer, and motion graphics designer based in Delhi, India. 2+ years of turning raw footage and bold ideas into cinematic, scroll-stopping stories.

My work lives at the intersection of post-production craft and graphic sensibility — from music video edits and brand films to kinetic title sequences and social content.

I believe the best edits are felt before they're fully seen. Every cut, every frame, every motion graphic in service of one thing: emotional impact.
